
# Mission Reflection
1. During this mission, the cloud platform that impressed me the most was **Google Cloud Platform (GCP)**. I was impressed by its strong capabilities in Artificial Intelligence, Machine Learning, data analytics, and Kubernetes. GCP also provides services such as Compute Engine, Cloud Storage, Cloud SQL, and Google Kubernetes Engine, which can support different types of applications and business requirements.

2. AWS, Azure, and GCP have many similarities because they all provide essential cloud computing services. All three platforms offer virtual machines, object storage, databases, networking, identity and access management, Kubernetes, and other services that allow organizations to build and manage applications in the cloud. They also provide scalable and flexible infrastructure, allowing businesses to increase or decrease resources depending on their needs.

3. Business requirements greatly influence cloud platform selection because different organizations have different priorities. For example, a company that already uses Microsoft products may benefit more from Azure, while an organization focused on AI and Kubernetes may prefer GCP. A business that needs a very broad selection of cloud services and global infrastructure may consider AWS.

4. Acting as a **Cloud Solutions Consultant** taught me that choosing a cloud provider is not simply about selecting the most popular platform. I learned that a consultant must first understand the client's budget, existing technologies, workload requirements, scalability needs, and business goals before recommending a platform. This helped me understand how cloud technologies can be matched to real-world business problems.

5. My **GitHub Cloud Computing Portfolio** also improved after completing this mission. I was able to organize my knowledge about AWS, Azure, and GCP through comparisons, service mappings, client recommendations, and decision matrices. These activities made my portfolio more informative and demonstrated that I can analyze cloud platforms and recommend appropriate solutions based on specific business requirements.
